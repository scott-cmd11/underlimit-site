UnderLimit Privacy Policy

Effective August 21, 2026

What UnderLimit collects

UnderLimit does not collect or transmit personal information, selected images or PDFs, usage data or diagnostics to the developer.

On-device processing

UnderLimit 1.3 processes selected images and PDFs, including image batches and supported combined PDF batches, in private temporary storage on your device. The original is never modified. Image compression creates a new JPEG copy. PDF shrinking creates a new flattened visual copy that removes searchable text, links, forms, annotations and other interactive behaviour.

Temporary files

Working copies are removed when you start over and during cleanup on the next launch. iOS may also remove temporary files when it needs storage.

Export control

A result leaves UnderLimit only when you choose Save to Photos or a destination from an image or PDF share action. Save to Photos requests add-only access and cannot read your photo library. The selected destination then handles the exported JPEG or PDF under its own privacy practices.

Accounts, tracking and third parties

UnderLimit has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud database or third-party software development kit. UnderLimit does not share app data with third parties.

Support requests

If you email for support, your email provider and ours process the information you choose to send. Support providers are used only where their safeguards provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law. Do not send sensitive images, PDFs or confidential information.

Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved, unless longer retention is required by law.

You may withdraw a support request or ask for deletion at any time by emailing scott@scotthazlitt.ai. The developer will delete the retained copy, subject to legal obligations and the provider’s backup cycle.

This website

This page uses no analytics, cookies or tracking. The developer does not receive or retain website logs. A hosting provider may temporarily process standard request information to deliver and secure the page under its published privacy and retention practices.

Children

UnderLimit does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If UnderLimit’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is released.
