UnderLimit Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

What UnderLimit collects

UnderLimit does not collect or transmit personal information, selected files, scanned pages, usage data or diagnostics to the developer.

On-device processing

UnderLimit 1.6 processes the files you select and the pages you scan in private temporary storage on your device. This covers images, PDFs, videos, audio recordings, Word and PowerPoint documents (DOCX and PPTX) and ZIP archives, including image batches, combined PDFs and several files sharing one size limit. There is no cloud processing. Cloud-provider files may need downloading through iOS first. Original files are never modified.

Image compression creates a new JPEG copy without source metadata, including GPS. PDF shrinking and visual conversion create a new flattened visual copy that removes searchable text, links, forms, annotations and other interactive behaviour. Video compression creates a new MP4 copy and audio compression a new M4A copy. Office compression changes only supported embedded images. Create ZIP packages files without changing their contents. Files that Fit several files keeps in their original format, and files packaged by Create ZIP, keep any metadata they already contain, such as location.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan document pages. iOS asks for your permission first. Scans are processed on this device and are not sent to the developer.

Temporary files

Working copies are removed when you start over and during cleanup on the next launch. iOS may also remove temporary files when it needs storage.

Export control

A result leaves UnderLimit only when you choose a save or share action. Save to Photos requests add-only access and cannot read your photo library. A share destination, including Files, then handles the exported file under its own privacy practices.

Accounts, tracking and third parties

UnderLimit has no account, subscription, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud database or third-party tracking SDK. UnderLimit does not share app data with third parties. ZIPFoundation handles archives locally without sending file contents anywhere.

Support requests

If you email for support, your email provider and ours process the information you choose to send. Support providers are used only where their safeguards provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable law. Do not send sensitive images, PDFs or confidential information.

Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after the request is resolved, unless longer retention is required by law.

You may withdraw a support request or ask for deletion at any time by emailing scott@scotthazlitt.ai. The developer will delete the retained copy, subject to legal obligations and the provider’s backup cycle.

This website

This page uses no analytics, cookies or tracking. The developer does not receive or retain website logs. A hosting provider may temporarily process standard request information to deliver and secure the page under its published privacy and retention practices.

Children

UnderLimit does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If UnderLimit’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is released.
