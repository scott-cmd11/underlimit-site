UnderLimit Support

UnderLimit 1.3 creates verified image and single-PDF copies to a decimal maximum from 10 KB through 100 MB. It reports “under your limit” only after measuring the finished file on disk. Some targets cannot be reached without an unreadable result. PDF combining has separate input limits, verifies output integrity and has no target-size guarantee.

Supported images

Image compression accepts JPEG, HEIC and PNG still images only. One image may be up to 250 MB, 50 million pixels and 32,768 pixels per edge; batch compression accepts 2–10 images with those limits applied separately to each image. Animated images and video are not supported.

UnderLimit creates a new sRGB JPEG. Transparent backgrounds become white, and source metadata, including GPS, is not retained. The original image is never modified. Verified batch results remain available if another image fails.

Supported PDFs

Single-PDF shrinking supports one supported, unencrypted, unsigned PDF up to 100 pages and 100 MB source input. The chosen output size is a maximum, not a guarantee: image-heavy PDFs may be rejected when their decoded image data is not safe to process on the device, some inline-image PDF formats are not accepted, and complex or unsupported interactive structures may be rejected.

UnderLimit creates a new flattened visual PDF. It keeps the page appearance but removes searchable text, links, forms, annotations and other interactive behaviour.

Combine PDFs

UnderLimit combines 2–10 supported PDFs after you review and reorder them. A combined batch is limited to 50 total pages and 100 MB of source data. Supported page content and HTTP or HTTPS links are preserved.

Encrypted, signed and unsupported interactive structures may be rejected. The original PDFs remain unchanged.

Troubleshooting

If UnderLimit cannot access an image, choose it again from Photos or Files; if it cannot access a PDF, choose it again from Files. If it reports that the source is too large, over the page limit or unsupported, use a smaller supported file or remove the protected or interactive feature in the source app.

Export

UnderLimit never modifies the original. For an image result, choose Save to Photos or share the JPEG to another destination. For a shrunk or combined PDF result, export the PDF through the system share sheet.

Privacy

UnderLimit processes images and PDFs locally on your iPhone. It has no account, cloud processing, analytics or tracking, and the developer does not collect app data.
