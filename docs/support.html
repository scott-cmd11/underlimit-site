UnderLimit Support

UnderLimit 1.6 brings seven tools together on one Home screen: Images, PDFs, Videos, Audio, Office documents, Fit several files and Create ZIP. Compression creates copies to a decimal maximum from 10 KB through 100 MB and reports success only after measuring the finished file. If a target cannot safely be reached, UnderLimit tells you. Your originals stay unchanged. Smaller files can lose detail or sound quality, and not every codec, document or size target is supported.

Supported images

Image compression accepts JPEG, HEIC and PNG still images. One image may be up to 250 MB, 50 million pixels and 32,768 pixels per edge; batch compression accepts 2–10 images with those limits applied separately to each image. For supported video files, use Videos.

UnderLimit creates a new sRGB JPEG. Transparent backgrounds become white, and source metadata, including GPS, is not retained. Verified batch results remain available if another image fails.

Supported PDFs

Single-PDF shrinking supports one supported, unencrypted, unsigned PDF up to 100 pages and 100 MB source input, subject to memory safety checks. The chosen output size is a maximum, not a guarantee: image-heavy PDFs may be rejected when their decoded image data is not safe to process on the device, some inline-image PDF formats are not accepted, and complex or unsupported interactive structures may be rejected.

UnderLimit creates a new flattened visual PDF. It keeps the page appearance but removes searchable text, links, forms, annotations and other interactive behaviour. Visual conversion may also remove accessibility information, so the copy may not work with VoiceOver.

Combine PDFs

UnderLimit combines 2–10 supported PDFs after you review and reorder them. A combined batch is limited to 50 total pages and 100 MB of source data. You can set an optional maximum size; if reaching it needs a flattened visual copy, UnderLimit asks for your confirmation before visual conversion. When you set a maximum, only verified results within that limit are offered for sharing.

Protected, signed and unsupported PDFs may be rejected. The original PDFs remain unchanged.

Scan documents

In PDFs, you can scan up to 30 document pages with your camera and review them before sharing. The camera is used only when you choose to scan.

Videos and audio

Videos accepts supported, playable MOV, MP4 or M4V files up to 2 GB and 8,192 pixels per edge, imported from Photos or Files. Compressed videos are standard-colour MP4 at up to 1080p and 30 fps; HDR and higher frame rates are converted. The copy keeps sound when the source has a compatible audio track.

Audio reduces supported recordings from Files into M4A copies that you can preview and share.

Office documents

Office documents reduces embedded JPEG and PNG images in DOCX and PPTX files while keeping text and document structure editable. Only supported embedded images change. Signed, encrypted and macro-enabled documents are not supported, and some documents cannot shrink enough. Preview important documents before sharing.

Fit several files and Create ZIP

Fit several files gives supported attachments one combined size budget. You can share them separately or as one ZIP within that limit. Supported files that already fit may be kept in their original format.

Create ZIP packages files into one archive without changing their contents. It does not promise a smaller file, and ZIPs of already-compressed photos or videos may be slightly larger.

Both tools accept up to 20 files totalling 2 GB.

Troubleshooting

If UnderLimit cannot access a file, choose it again: images and videos from Photos or Files, and other files from Files. Cloud-provider files may need downloading through iOS first. If UnderLimit reports that the source is too large, over the page limit or unsupported, use a smaller supported file or remove the protected or interactive feature in the source app.

Export

UnderLimit never modifies the original. Preview the copy and compare the original and new sizes before sharing. For an image result, choose Save to Photos or share the JPEG to another destination. For PDF, scan, video, audio, Office and ZIP results, use the system share sheet to choose a destination such as Files.

Privacy

Core processing happens locally on your iPhone, including scanned pages. UnderLimit has no account, subscription, advertising, analytics, tracking or cloud processing, and the developer does not collect your files, usage or diagnostics.
