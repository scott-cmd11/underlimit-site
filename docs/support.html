UnderLimit Support

UnderLimit 1.0 (1) compresses JPEG, HEIC and PNG still images to a decimal target from 10 KB through 100 MB.

Supported images

One still image up to 250 MB, 50 million pixels and 32,768 pixels per edge. Animated images, video and PDF are not supported.

Troubleshooting

If UnderLimit cannot access an image, choose it again from Photos or Files. If it reports that the image is too large or invalid, use a smaller valid still image.

Export

UnderLimit never modifies the original. After compression, choose Export JPEG and select a destination in the system share sheet.

Privacy

UnderLimit processes images locally and does not collect app data.
